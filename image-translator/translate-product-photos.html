Translate product photos on AliExpress, Taobao and 1688

On marketplaces, the important details — sizes, materials, what's in the box — are often written on the photos themselves, in Chinese, Japanese or Korean. Chrome's page translation doesn't touch text inside images. Image Translator does: right-click a photo, and the translation appears over it, in a side panel next to the page.

How to translate a product photo

  1. Install Image Translator — free, no account.
  2. Open the product page in Chrome: AliExpress, Taobao, Tmall, 1688, Temu, Pinduoduo, Amazon Japan or any other shop.
  3. Right-click the photo and choose “Translate image”. Or press Alt+Shift+S (⌥⇧S on Mac) and select a part of the page — a size chart, for example.
  4. The translation is drawn over each label in place, so numbers and sizes stay where they were. The text blocks are also listed below the picture, with the original, and can be copied.

Tips

  • Open the photo at its largest size in the product gallery before right-clicking. The extension takes the image file the page loads, at that file's own resolution — the bigger the photo, the smaller the print it can read.
  • Some shops draw photos as backgrounds or on a canvas, and the right-click menu doesn't offer “Translate image”. Select the area with Alt+Shift+S (⌥⇧S on Mac) — it works on anything on screen.
  • Product descriptions are often one very tall image. Scroll to the part you need and press Alt+Shift+A to translate what is visible.
  • For the page text itself — titles and descriptions written as text — use Chrome's built-in page translation. Image Translator is for the text inside pictures.

Your shopping stays private

Nothing about what you look at or buy leaves your computer: the photo is recognized on your graphics card and translated by Chrome's built-in on-device translator. No account, no server, no tracking.

Limitations

  • Very small print on low-resolution or heavily compressed photos may be misread — double-check sizes and numbers before you order.
  • Stylized logo lettering is often not recognized; handwriting is recognized only partially.

Frequently asked questions

How do I translate Chinese text in a Taobao or 1688 product image?

Right-click the image and choose “Translate image”, or press Alt+Shift+S (⌥⇧S on Mac) and select the part of the page you need. The translation appears over the picture in the side panel.

Can it translate size charts?

Yes. The translation is drawn over each label in place, so the numbers stay where they were; the recognized text is also listed below the picture, with the original.

Does it work when I am logged in to the shop?

Yes. It works with whatever your Chrome shows. It does not use your account or read the page — only the picture you choose.
